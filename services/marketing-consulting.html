Our 6-Stage Strategic Consulting Process

How we structure marketing advisory to generate rapid clarity and long-term pipeline momentum.

01

Discovery & Audit

Comprehensive audit of current marketing spend, pipeline velocity, lead conversion drop-offs, and brand perception.

02

ICP Definition

Defining firmographic parameters, job titles, pain points, and decision criteria for top revenue-generating accounts.

03

Positioning Strategy

Crafting a distinctive value proposition that sets your brand apart from entrenched category competitors.

04

Campaign Architecture

Structuring the multi-channel integration between physical activations, digital media, webinars, and outbound cadences.

05

Execution Roadmap

Step-by-step rollout plan with budget allocations, technology stack recommendations, and internal milestone checkpoints.

06

Attribution & Review

Setting up closed-loop CRM reporting to track SQLs, sales pipeline contribution, customer acquisition costs, and payback cycles.
