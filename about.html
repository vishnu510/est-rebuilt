Who We Are

Our Foundation

An Integrated Marketing Ecosystem Built for High-Stakes Business Goals

Founded in 2018 by corporate marketing veteran Satya Narayan (MBA, Ex-Religare, Ex-HPE), EST Brand Services was created to solve a fundamental industry problem: traditional agencies were either theoretical strategists who couldn't execute on the ground, or production vendors who didn't understand enterprise business objectives.

EST operates as a unified marketing partner. We align marketing consulting, ICP demand generation, and digital advertising with immersive experiential activations, corporate conferences, and nationwide BTL rollouts.
